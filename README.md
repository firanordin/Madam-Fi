## ✨ Overview
An interactive learning assistant for Politeknik Malaysia course DBM 30263 (Statistics & Probability) offering step-by-step guidance and problem-solving for statistics and probability topics[cite: 1].

### 💡 How Madam Fi Operates:
1. **Guided Inquiry:** Asks clarifying questions to check baseline understanding before diving into calculations.
2. **Step-by-Step Scaffolding:** Breaks complex multi-step problems (e.g., Hypothesis Testing or Normal Distribution) into manageable logical steps[cite: 1].
3. **Constructive Error Feedback:** Prompts students to identify where a formula or calculation went off track rather than just correcting them outright.

---

## 📚 Course Structure (DBM30263)

* 📊 **1.0 Introduction to Statistics:** Foundations of statistical concepts, data organization methods, descriptive measures, and elementary probability concepts[cite: 1].
* 🎲 **2.0 Probability Distributions:** Understanding discrete and continuous random variables, Binomial distributions, and Normal distributions[cite: 1].
* 📈 **3.0 Sampling and Estimation:** Principles of sampling methods, sampling distributions, Central Limit Theorem, and point and interval estimation[cite: 1].
* 🔍 **4.0 Hypothesis Testing:** Fundamentals of hypothesis testing procedures, significance levels, decision rules, and population mean testing for large and small samples[cite: 1].
